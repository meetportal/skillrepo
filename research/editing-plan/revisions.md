# What the first build changed

The plan was written before any full render existed. Building and measuring the first reel overturned several of its numbers. Each change below follows the precedence rule in [decisions.md](decisions.md): a measurement on our own render beat a repo default or a guide value. The current values live in the linked skills.

| Topic | Plan | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audio splice | 30 ms afade out and in at every splice | 16 ms equal-power (sine/cosine) crossfade centred on the splice, 8 ms of handle each side ([`reel-cut`](../../skills/reel-cut/SKILL.md)) | Fade-out/fade-in pairs left 40 to 60 ms holes 15 to 25 dB under room tone. Room tone must never drop at a splice; a splice check now measures it |
| Denoiser | Tracking `afftdn` (`tn=1`, `nf=-58`) plus a gate | No denoiser on a quiet room (floor -55 dBFS or lower, SNR 35 dB or more); a soft downward expander instead. When a stationary noise needs FFT reduction, `afftdn` with a fixed profile sampled from room tone (`tn=0`), or DeepFilterNet ([`voice-clean`](../../skills/voice-clean/SKILL.md)) | Tracking `afftdn` nearly doubled pause kurtosis (musical noise) and re-tracked across every splice. On a quiet room a denoiser removed about 2 dB that compression gave straight back |
| Pass criterion for the voice | Lowest absolute floor after normalisation | The background must not get louder relative to the voice than it was in the raw cut, measured at -14 LUFS | Compression lowers speech and leaves quiet frames alone, so after normalisation room tone and breaths come up. A chain that fails this is worse than no processing |
| Voice peaks | Left to the mix-bus limiter | A look-ahead peak limiter at the end of the voice chain, holding peaks about 14 dB above the voice loudness | A close-mic voice measured about 19 dB peak to loudness and the mix needed nearly 4 dB of bus limiting |
| Filter latency | Not considered | Each stage's delay is measured and removed (`afftdn` delays 1200 samples at 48 kHz, `arnndn` 480, an `alimiter` its attack) | The master came out about 10 ms late against the picture |
| Bed level and duck | About 16 LU under the voice, sidechain duck of 6 dB | 10 LU under the voice when unducked, with a duck keyed by the word timings; sidechain kept as a fallback ([`reel-sound`](../../skills/reel-sound/SKILL.md)) | A sidechain duck moved the bed 5 to 10 dB inside a sentence (pumping); the word-keyed duck keeps it under about 2 dB |
| SFX levels | Peak targets in dBFS per category | Loudness targets in LU relative to the voice, with the peak only as a safety cap | A 30 ms pop or a 0.35 s impact has a large peak-to-loudness ratio, so peak targets left short sounds 5 to 6 LU too quiet |
| SFX source | A third-party CC0 kit only | A seeded generated kit is available alongside any vetted CC0 kit | No licence questions, and each generated file carries an exact anchor for alignment |
| Loudness pass 1 | loudnorm's own measurement | `ebur128` (the same meter as the delivery gate) | loudnorm's pass-1 reading drifted up to 0.4 LU on short files |
| Word timing | Large-model ASR with energy-snapped edges | Force-aligned words, with onset gates ([`reel-ingest`](../../skills/reel-ingest/SKILL.md)) | Energy snapping alone cannot place onsets inside continuous speech, and it reads a breath before a phrase as speech. Onsets now need to be accurate to one video frame |
| Logo | No persistent logo; brand only in title styling, logo pop and end card | A persistent top-centre brand mark (y 120 to 220) for the whole talking section, hidden under the end card ([`reel-motion`](../../skills/reel-motion/SKILL.md)) | Now a hard rule in `reel-director`. The plan's objection was to a corner bug colliding with platform buttons; a top-centre mark inside its own reserved zone clears them |
| Soft frames at cuts | Cover with a takeover | Hold the nearest sharp frame for 2 to 4 frames at a cut or at frame 0; longer holds under speech become mandatory cover windows ([`reel-cut`](../../skills/reel-cut/SKILL.md)) | A short soft run at a cut or at the first frame is fixed in the cut, without spending a takeover on it |
| Camera kicks | A 2-frame, 4 px shake on stamps | A defined kick (sign-flipping decay over about 7 frames, sub-pixel offsets, scale raised just enough that no edge shows) | A shake moves the frame, so the scale has to cover the offset or an edge shows; the kick computes that |
| 3D | Not in the plan | 3D inserts as punctuation for physical metaphors, 2 to 3 moments and 4 to 8 s per 45 to 60 s reel ([`reel-3d`](../../skills/reel-3d/SKILL.md)) | Added after the plan as a separate stage |
| Caption style | One look | A `standard` style (the plan's) and an optional `kinetic` style with an active-word pill ([`reel-captions`](../../skills/reel-captions/SKILL.md)) | Added as an option; the standard style stays the default |

## Values the build confirmed

These plan values survived measurement unchanged and are still the defaults: the three-stage boundary, one explicit range conversion, the single `filter_complex` cut, no `-shortest`, no speed change, the cut rules for pauses and pads, the 0.28 s breath after list markers, the 1.20 total-scale cap, the three layout modes and the safe box, the caption page rules and the 1-frame lead, -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP / LRA 7 on the delivered file, the export spec, the critic loop with at most 3 passes, and the five-timestamp human listen.
